## Georgia Institute of Technology

### CSE/ISyE 6740 — Computational Data Analysis

An introduction to the computational techniques and theory behind machine learning
and data science. Topics include clustering, dimensionality reduction, density
estimation, regression, decision trees, support vector machines, convex optimization,
kernel methods, neural networks, and gradient descent. Advanced topics include
CNNs, GNNs, autoencoders, diffusion models, Markov models, and reinforcement learning.

### CSE 8803 — AI for Social Impact

Explore how AI can address societal challenges in healthcare, public health,
education, inequality, climate, environmental sustainability, and urban planning.
The course connects technical methods with interdisciplinary perspectives on
building systems that benefit society.

Topics include game theory, Stackelberg models, linear programming, mechanism
design, influence maximization, multi-armed and restless bandits, machine learning
for the environment, and the implications of LLMs, transformers, and diffusion models.
